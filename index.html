Delulu Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Delulu ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Delulu mobile application.

By using Delulu, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Delulu may collect or process the following information:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address.
  • Authentication information provided through email/password sign-in or Google Sign-In.
  • Information required to create and participate in teams, including team identifiers and player information.
  • Game-related information such as team codes, game progress, scores, match information, and player information.
  • Contact-derived information when you voluntarily enable contact synchronization.
  • Voice/audio information when you voluntarily use the voice-chat feature.
  • Information that you voluntarily provide while using the application.

2. Account and Authentication

Delulu allows users to create and access accounts using email and password authentication. If you choose to use Google Sign-In, Google authentication services are used to authenticate your account.

Depending on the authentication method you use, Delulu may process information such as your name, email address, and account authentication identifiers.

3. Contacts

Delulu provides an optional contact-synchronization feature that can help users find friends who use the service.

If you enable this feature, Delulu may access contacts on your device after obtaining the required permission.

Contact identifiers are processed locally into hashed values before being used for friend-discovery purposes. Contact synchronization is optional and is not required for basic gameplay.

Delulu does not require access to your contacts for normal account creation, login, or basic gameplay.

4. Voice Chat

Delulu provides an optional real-time voice-chat feature for players.

If you use voice chat, the application may request microphone permission and transmit audio through third-party voice communication services used by the application, including Agora, in order to provide real-time communication between players.

Microphone access is used only when required for the voice-chat functionality and is subject to the permissions granted by the user.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide multiplayer and team features.
  • Allow users to find and invite friends.
  • Store game progress and game-related information.
  • Provide real-time voice communication when enabled.
  • Maintain, operate, and improve the application.
  • Protect the service against misuse and security issues.
  • Respond to user requests and support inquiries.

6. Firebase

Delulu uses Firebase services to provide features such as authentication, database storage, and related application functionality.

Depending on the features you use, information such as account information, user profiles, team information, and game-related data may be processed through Firebase services.

Firebase services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

7. Google Sign-In

Delulu provides Google Sign-In as an optional authentication method.

When you choose Google Sign-In, Google authentication services process information associated with your Google account as necessary to authenticate you. Delulu may receive information such as your name, email address, profile information, and authentication identifiers made available through the authentication process.

You may use email and password authentication instead of Google Sign-In.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

Delulu uses third-party services that are necessary to provide certain application features. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

These services may include:

  • Google Firebase for authentication and database services.
  • Google Sign-In for optional account authentication.
  • Agora for real-time voice communication.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate application features, provide authentication, store application data, or provide voice communication services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Delulu against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

11. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's features, maintain user accounts, provide requested services, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, it may be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

12. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Delulu account and associated personal information by contacting us using the contact information provided below.

Upon receiving a valid deletion request, we will process the request and delete applicable account information, subject to information that we may be required or permitted to retain by applicable law, security requirements, or legitimate operational purposes.

13. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Delulu may request certain permissions from your device, including:

  • Microphone: required for the optional voice-chat feature.
  • Contacts: optional permission used for the contact-synchronization and friend-discovery feature.
  • Internet: required for authentication, multiplayer functionality, database access, and other online features.

You can control applicable permissions through your device settings. Some features may not function if the required permission is denied.

14. Children's Privacy

Delulu is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application in circumstances where such collection was not appropriate, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, our practices, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
