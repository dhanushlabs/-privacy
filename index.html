Delulu Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Delulu ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Delulu mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Delulu may collect:

  • Account information such as your name and email address.
  • Information required to create and participate in teams.
  • Game-related information such as team codes, game progress, scores, and player information.
  • Contact information or contact-derived data when you voluntarily enable contact-sync features.
  • Information that you voluntarily provide while using the app.

2. Contacts

If you enable the contact-sync feature, Delulu may access contacts on your device after obtaining the required permission.

Contact information may be processed in a privacy-preserving form, such as hashed contact identifiers, to help identify friends who use the service. Contact access is not required for basic gameplay.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide multiplayer and team features.
  • Allow users to find and invite friends.
  • Save game progress and game-related information.
  • Maintain and improve the application.
  • Protect the service against misuse and security issues.

4. Firebase

Delulu uses Firebase services to provide features such as data storage, authentication, and application functionality. Information processed through Firebase is handled according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

5. Cloudinary

Where applicable, Delulu may use Cloudinary to store and deliver images or other media uploaded through the application.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as Firebase and Cloudinary.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Delulu. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's features, maintain accounts, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve disputes.

9. Account and Data Deletion

If you want your account or associated personal information deleted, please contact us using the email address below. We will process deletion requests subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Delulu is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
